**OGITO** (Open Geospatial Interactive TOol) supports collaborative spatial planning on maptables: large horizontal touch screens that a group of stakeholders can use together.

* Co-designed with planners and stakeholders using human-centred design and agile development.
* Used for community mapping in Sumatra, Indonesia, and for noise action planning.
* Code on [GitHub](https://github.com/rosaguilar/ogito); dataset on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7415088).
* Papers: [CEUS 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2020.101591), [Journal of Urban Technology 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/10630732.2023.2190705), and the [PhD thesis](https://doi.org/10.3990/1.9789036554206).
