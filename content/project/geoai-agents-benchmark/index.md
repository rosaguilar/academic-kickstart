AI agents are starting to operate GIS software on their own. How reliable are they on real geospatial tasks?

I lead the design of a benchmark that evaluates AI agents on geospatial tasks in QGIS. The [benchmark questions](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23208192) are openly available on Zenodo.
